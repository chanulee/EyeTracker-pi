근거: [OpenCV 4.11 AVFoundation 소스](https://github.com/opencv/opencv/blob/4.11.0/modules/videoio/src/cap_avfoundation_mac.mm), [속성의 백엔드 의존성](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d4/d15/group__videoio__flags__base.html).
